How can I vote?

Your voice counts too, no matter the distance.

2026-2027 Local Electoral Process

LNERE registration period From September 1, 2026 to February 25, 2027

The Electoral and Citizen Participation Institute of the State of Guerrero (IEPC) guarantees the right of Guerrero citizens living abroad to vote remotely.

  1. 1

    Get or renew your INE voter ID*

    *If your voter ID is still valid, you can skip this step.

  2. 2

    Register in the LNERE

    LNERE: List of Voters Residing Abroad.

  3. 3

    Choose how to vote: online, by mail or in person

    Dates are different for each method.

Yes. The National Electoral Institute (INE) is authorized to issue the Voter ID Card at Mexican embassies and consulates abroad.

Citizens who wish to vote from abroad must request registration in the List of Voters Residing Abroad and choose their voting method (online, by mail or in person) within this period: September 1, 2026 to February 25, 2027.

Registration is done on the official INE website: votoextranjero.mx.

1. Prepare your documents (three in total):

  • Proof of nationality (birth certificate or another document proving Mexican nationality).
  • Photo ID (passport, high-security consular ID, among others).
  • Proof of address abroad.

See the full list at votoextranjero.mx/documentos-credencial.

2. Book an appointment at your consulate: call 1-877-MEXITEL (639-4835) from the United States and Canada, or go to citas.sre.gob.mx.

3. Track your Voter ID. It is sent to your address by courier four weeks after your application. After three delivery attempts it will be held for up to 30 days at the nearest pickup office; if you do not collect it, it will be returned to Mexico and you will need to request a new shipment.

4. Activate your Voter ID in the INE tracking system using the folio number on your receipt, your date of birth and some details from your new card.

Once the requirements are verified, you are added to the List of Voters Residing Abroad; if you were previously registered in the voter registry, you are removed from the section for citizens residing in Mexico.

When the Electoral Process ends, the lists of voters residing abroad expire and citizens are re-registered in the voter list of the electoral section that corresponds to their address in Mexico (article 335, paragraphs 2 and 4, LGIPE).
